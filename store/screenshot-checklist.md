# Optimize — Store Screenshot Checklist

Use LG Seller Lounge required sizes and formats for screenshots.

Capture checklist
- Home/viewing screen with a channel playing.
- Channel list open.
- EPG grid open (if EPG data present).
- Settings panel open (playlist management).
- Info bar visible over playback.
- Empty state (no playlists) with guidance to open Settings.

Notes
- Use the TV's native resolution (typically 1920x1080).
- Avoid showing any third-party branding or copyrighted channel logos unless you have rights.
