# Optimize — Screenshot Capture Steps

Goal: capture store screenshots at the TV's native resolution (typically 1920x1080).

Option A: webOS screenshot (recommended)
1. Open the screen you want to capture (e.g., channel list).
2. Use the TV remote: press and hold the "Home" button, then select "Screenshot" (exact wording may vary by webOS version).
3. Save the screenshot to the TV.
4. Transfer the images to your computer (USB drive or network transfer).

Option B: phone camera (fallback)
1. Reduce reflections (dim lights, avoid glare).
2. Hold the camera level and centered.
3. Use highest resolution and no flash.
4. Crop to the screen edges.

Required shots (from store/screenshot-checklist.md)
- Viewing screen with playback
- Channel list
- EPG grid
- Settings panel
- Info bar overlay
- Empty state (no playlists)
