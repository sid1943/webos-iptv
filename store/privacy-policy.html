Optimize Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 7, 2026

Optimize is a playlist player for LG webOS TVs. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information.

Information we process

  • Playlist URLs you enter in the app. These are stored locally on your TV in app storage.
  • Playback requests made by the app to the URLs you provide.

Information we do not collect

  • No account data, names, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • No analytics, advertising IDs, or tracking data.
  • No location data.
  • No device identifiers are collected or transmitted.
  • No cookies or web beacons are used.

Local storage

  • The app stores settings, playlists, favorites, and channel history locally on your TV.
  • You can remove this data by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Third-party content

  • Optimize only accesses media from URLs you provide.
  • You are responsible for ensuring you have the rights to access and play those streams.

Children’s privacy

  • Optimize is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information.

Changes to this policy

  • If we update this policy, we will change the effective date above.
